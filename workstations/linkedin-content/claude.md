# LinkedIn Content Workstation

**Purpose:** plan and draft LinkedIn / dev-community posts documenting Caleb's Cowork build — what was tried, what was true, what broke, what the guides don't tell you.

**When to use:** any session about content ideas, drafting posts, or reviewing the build log for material.

**Inputs Claude needs:** `build-log.md` in this folder (raw material), plus whatever happened recently in the workspace worth writing about.

**Typical flow:**
1. Review recent build-log entries together; pick an angle.
2. Claude drafts in the outputs folder; Caleb reacts; iterate.
3. Finished drafts get copied here under `resources/drafts/`.
4. After posting, log what resonated (engagement, comments) to built-in memory.

**Standing rules:**
- Angle: practitioner-tests-the-advice, written from a new-to-dev perspective. Findings over summaries — "here's what was actually true when I tried it" beats restating someone's guide.
- Credit sources by name (e.g., Jeff Su) when building on their material.
- Every notable workspace finding gets a dated build-log entry when it happens, not reconstructed later.
- No fabricated metrics or results — only things that actually happened in this workspace.
